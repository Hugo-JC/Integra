## Important migration corrections

- Fixed headline sizes, spacing ratios, grid ratios, radius lists and accent percentages from the source project are historical suggestions, not universal validation constraints.
- A spacing scale or token system must follow the target project's real conventions and user needs.
- Do not ban borders, pills, gradients, card layouts, dark themes, serif fonts or other visual patterns universally. Evaluate their role, accessibility and product context.
- A split hero, bento grid, social-proof section or large display headline is optional. Select structure from the primary task and the content actually available.
- Never create fake testimonials, customer logos, performance metrics, product capabilities, certifications, legal claims or case-study results to complete a pattern.
- A heuristic or anti-pattern signal is not a confirmed defect without evidence and user impact.
- WCAG requirements must be evaluated against the relevant criterion and actual rendered state; an approximate contrast calculation or token value alone is not proof of conformance.
