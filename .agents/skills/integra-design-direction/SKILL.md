# Integra Design Direction

Start with product intent, users, primary task and constraints—not a fashionable style. Follow docs/ai-context-contract.md whenever presets, references or quality rules influence a decision.

## Workflow
1. Inspect routes, components, tokens, fonts, assets, screenshots, tests and product docs. Preserve established identity unless redesign is requested.
2. Extract audience, primary job, content hierarchy, key action, trust needs, platform, viewports and accessibility target. Separate facts, constraints, preferences, assumptions and unknowns.
3. Write a one-sentence visual thesis and choose 2–4 supporting traits. Adjectives alone are not a direction.
4. Match presets as candidates. Explain the match using the actual brief; check variant conditions; resolve pattern IDs before use; reject mismatches. Never apply a preset wholesale or treat keyword matching as proof of fit.
5. Define semantic tokens for surfaces, text, borders, accent, focus, status, spacing, type, radius, elevation and motion. Reuse existing tokens first.
6. Plan composition, information order, density, grid and responsive transformations before decoration.
7. Specify hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, empty, error, success and reduced-motion states where relevant.
8. Compare at least two plausible directions when the choice is consequential; state trade-offs and remove decoration without a product purpose.
9. For every external reference, state the exact observed principle, source ID, relevance and limitation. Do not claim inspection based on a URL alone.
10. Hand off thesis, selected/rejected preset rationale, tokens, layout rules, component principles, responsive behavior, accessibility constraints, assumptions and acceptance checks.

## Anti-generic safeguards
- Do not default to purple/blue gradients, giant centered hero copy, glassmorphism, pills everywhere, nested cards or icon tiles above every heading.
- Do not ban patterns universally; use them when brand, content or interaction justifies them.
- Keep real product imagery and truthful content ahead of decorative blobs or invented metrics.
- Integra blue belongs to Integra tooling, not every customer palette.
- Do not invent audience research, product capabilities, testimonials, metrics or compliance claims.

## Acceptance
The primary task is obvious; composition is distinctive and clear; tokens are semantic; mobile/desktop behavior is described; keyboard, contrast and reduced motion are considered; claims are sourced or marked as assumptions; preset and reference choices are traceable.
