# Reference and Research Policy

## Source types
- Official design-system documentation: component behavior, token architecture, accessibility and implementation guidance.
- Pattern libraries and galleries: broad comparison of visual patterns and composition.
- Product screenshots and live sites: observed output, not proof of usability or source implementation.
- User-provided references: high-priority signals, subject to explicit project constraints.

## Investigation protocol
1. State the design question and context.
2. Record URL, owner, capture date and why the source is relevant.
3. Inspect more than one example before concluding a pattern is a convention.
4. Distinguish direct observation from inference.
5. Extract transferable principles: hierarchy, density, affordance, motion, responsive behavior and content strategy.
6. Record alternatives, confidence and limitations.
7. Verify the proposed pattern in the target product.

## Anti-copying rules
- Never reproduce an entire site, screenshot, brand identity or distinctive composition.
- Do not reuse protected imagery, logos, copy or proprietary design assets without permission.
- Use references to understand a design problem and learn a principle, then produce an original solution.

## Inspired by
- [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable): command-oriented design workflows, automatic design checks, anti-pattern detection and iterative browser preview. Integra adopts the workflow concepts but implements its own rules and tools.
- [REA](https://github.com/morluto/rea): investigation driven by hypotheses, evidence and stated limitations. Integra adapts the evidence discipline to design research rather than copying reverse-engineering code.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): normative accessibility reference.
- [WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/): interaction patterns and semantics.
- [Material Design](https://m3.material.io/), [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/), [Fluent 2](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/): system-level guidance, not visual templates.

Original AI Design Engine references are preserved under `source-library/`; see `data/design-library/manifest.json` for source attribution.
